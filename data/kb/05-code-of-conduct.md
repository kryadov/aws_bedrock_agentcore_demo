# Quibblefrost Widgetworks — Code of Conduct (summary)

Owner: Legal and People Team. Effective: 1 January 2026.

## Respect
- Harassment, discrimination and bullying of any kind are not tolerated, online or offline.
- Report incidents to your manager, your HR partner or anonymously via the **EthicsPoint hotline** (ethics.quibblefrost.example, phone 1-800-555-0199).
- Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.

## Confidentiality
- Salary information of other employees, performance reviews and disciplinary cases are **confidential**; only HR and the direct management chain may access them.
- Internal project codenames and unreleased product plans must not be shared outside the company.
- Customer data is handled only per the Data Protection Policy.

## Gifts and conflicts of interest
- Gifts from vendors or customers above **$100** must be declared to Legal and usually declined.
- Side jobs are allowed if they do not compete with Quibblefrost Widgetworks; declare them to your manager.
- Relationships with a direct report must be disclosed to HR.

## Consequences
- Violations may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- HR does not give individual legal advice; employees may consult an external lawyer.
