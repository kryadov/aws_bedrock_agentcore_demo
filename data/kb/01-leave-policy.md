# Quibblefrost Widgetworks — Leave Policy

Owner: People Team (hr@quibblefrost.example). Effective: 1 January 2026.

## Annual vacation
- Full-time employees get **25 working days** of paid vacation per calendar year.
- After 5 years of service: **28 days**. Part-time employees accrue pro rata.
- Vacation accrues monthly (25 / 12 days per month). New joiners may take accrued days after probation (3 months).
- Up to **5 unused days** carry over to the next year and must be used by **31 March**; the rest expires.
- Requests go through Workday at least **2 weeks** in advance for absences of 3+ days; the manager approves within 3 working days.
- Company-wide shutdown: 24 December – 1 January (does not count against vacation).

## Sick leave
- Up to **10 paid sick days** per year without a doctor's note; from the 3rd consecutive day a note is required.
- Long-term sickness (over 10 days): full salary for 8 weeks, then 70% for up to 26 weeks.
- Report sickness to your manager before 10:00 local time and mark it in Workday.

## Parental leave
- Primary caregiver: **20 weeks** at full pay. Secondary caregiver: **6 weeks** at full pay.
- Available after 6 months of employment; can be split into up to 3 blocks within 18 months of the birth or adoption.
- Notify HR at least 8 weeks before the planned start.

## Other paid leave
- Bereavement: 5 days (immediate family), 2 days (extended family).
- Moving house: 1 day per year. Jury duty / civic duty: as required by law.
- Volunteering: 2 days per year for registered charities.

## Unpaid leave and sabbatical
- Unpaid leave up to 30 days per year, subject to manager and HR approval.
- Sabbatical: after 4 years of service, up to 3 months unpaid; the role is guaranteed on return.
