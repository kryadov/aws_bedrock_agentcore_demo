# Quibblefrost Widgetworks — Remote Work and Equipment

Owner: People Team and IT (it-help@quibblefrost.example). Effective: 1 January 2026.

## Hybrid model
- Default: **hybrid**, at least **2 office days per week** (Tuesday and Thursday are team days).
- Fully remote contracts are possible for roles approved by a VP; the employee must live in a country where Quibblefrost Widgetworks has an entity (US, UK, Germany, Poland).
- Working from abroad (outside your home country): up to **20 working days per year**, request via Workday 2 weeks ahead.
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00–15:00 in the team's home time zone.

## Home office stipend
- One-time home office allowance: **$1,000** in the first 6 months (desk, chair, monitor, headset).
- Monthly internet and phone allowance: **$50**, paid with salary.

## Company equipment
- Standard laptop: MacBook Pro 14" (engineering) or MacBook Air 13" (other roles). Linux laptops on request for engineering.
- Laptop refresh every **3 years**; earlier only if broken.
- Report lost or stolen equipment to IT within **24 hours** at it-help@quibblefrost.example.
- Equipment is returned within 5 working days after the last working day.

## Security basics
- Full-disk encryption, screen lock after 5 minutes and Okta Verify MFA are mandatory.
- Never store customer data on personal devices or personal cloud storage.
