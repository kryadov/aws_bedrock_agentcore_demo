# Quibblefrost Widgetworks — Onboarding and IT Access

Owner: IT (it-help@quibblefrost.example) and People Team.

## First day
- The laptop is shipped to the home address 3 working days before the start date.
- Log in with the temporary password from the welcome email, then enrol in **Okta Verify** (MFA).
- Accounts created automatically: Google Workspace, Slack, Workday, Expensify, Navan, Jira and Confluence.
- The onboarding buddy reaches out on Slack on day 1.

## First week
- Complete mandatory trainings in Workday Learning within **14 days**: security awareness, code of conduct, data protection.
- Engineers request GitHub and AWS access via the #it-access Slack channel; production access needs manager approval and expires after 90 days.

## Probation
- Probation lasts **3 months** with a check-in at 30, 60 and 90 days.
- Notice period during probation: 2 weeks; after probation: 1 month (US employees are at-will).

## Help
- IT helpdesk: #it-help Slack channel or it-help@quibblefrost.example, Monday–Friday 08:00–18:00 ET.
- Password reset: self-service at okta.quibblefrost.example.
