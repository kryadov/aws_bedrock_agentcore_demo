# Quibblefrost Widgetworks — Business Travel and Expenses

Owner: Finance (finance@quibblefrost.example). Effective: 1 January 2026.

## Booking
- Book all flights and hotels through **Navan**; bookings outside Navan are reimbursed only with Finance approval.
- Flights under **6 hours**: economy. Over 6 hours: premium economy. Business class requires VP approval.
- Book at least **14 days** in advance where possible.
- Hotels: up to **$220 per night** (US), **$300 per night** in New York, San Francisco, London and Tokyo.

## Per diem
- Meals while travelling: **$75 per day** (domestic), **$95 per day** (international). No receipts needed for per diem.
- Client entertainment is not covered by per diem: submit receipts and attendee names.

## Ground transport
- Taxi or ride-share to and from airports is reimbursed. Rental cars: compact class, only when cheaper than taxis.
- Personal car mileage: **$0.67 per mile**.

## Expense claims
- Submit claims in **Expensify** within **30 days** of the expense; claims older than 90 days are rejected.
- Receipts are required for any single item above **$25**.
- Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run after manager approval.
- Not reimbursable: alcohol without clients, minibar, fines, seat or class upgrades, personal entertainment.

## Corporate card
- Employees who travel more than 4 times a year may request an Amex corporate card (limit $5,000 per month).
- Personal use of the corporate card is not allowed.
