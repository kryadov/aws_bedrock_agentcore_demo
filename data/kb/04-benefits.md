# Quibblefrost Widgetworks — Benefits (US)

Owner: People Team (benefits@quibblefrost.example). Plan year: 1 January – 31 December 2026.

## Health
- Medical, dental and vision through Aetna: Quibblefrost Widgetworks pays **100%** of employee premiums and **80%** for dependants.
- Enrolment: within **30 days** of the start date or during open enrolment (November). Life events (marriage, birth) allow changes within 30 days.
- Mental health: 12 free therapy sessions per year through Spring Health.

## Retirement
- 401(k) with **4% company match** (100% of the first 4% of salary), immediately vested.

## Wellbeing and learning
- Wellness allowance: **$75 per month** (gym, sports, meditation apps).
- Learning budget: **$1,500 per year** for courses, conferences and books; needs manager approval above $300.
- Conference attendance: 3 days per year of paid time, in addition to vacation.

## Other
- Life insurance: 2× annual salary. Short- and long-term disability insurance included.
- Employee referral bonus: **$3,000** paid after the new hire passes probation.
- Commuter benefit: up to $300 per month pre-tax.

Employees outside the US: see the country supplement from your local HR partner.
